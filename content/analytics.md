This site includes local-only analytics to help surface your most-used tools.
Every HTML tool loads a small script that increments a per-tool counter stored in your browser’s localStorage.
Nothing is sent to a server and nothing leaves your device.

Open <a href="/analytics.html">Analytics</a> to review what has been tracked, export the JSON, or clear it.
The page also includes a “clear all tool local data” option, which wipes localStorage for this site.

<div>

## FAQ

**Where is the data stored?**

In your browser’s localStorage for this site only.

**Does anything get sent to a server?**

No. The tracking script runs locally and never transmits data.

**What gets recorded?**

The tool page path, page title, a view count, and the last access time.

**How do I remove it?**

Use the Analytics page to clear just analytics data, or clear all tool local data if you want a full wipe.

**Can I export it?**

Yes, the Analytics page can export or copy the JSON.
